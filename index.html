Designer & Photographer

Human-centered design for a more meaningful world.

I believe design should be human-centered. I'm most drawn to designing for humans to interact with technology intuitively and meaningfully, so that we can spend more time and intentionality towards things that actually matter. The problems I want to work on are designing more inclusive technologies, and working on improving technology for children in education and development.
